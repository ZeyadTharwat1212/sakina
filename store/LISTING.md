## Description (en)
Sakina is a calm daily companion for the Quran, prayer and adhkar — works offline, no account, collects no data.

• The printed Madani mushaf (King Fahd Complex fonts), ayah view and colour tajweed.
• Listen and follow: 200+ reciters, word-by-word highlighting on the mushaf page, compact offline downloads.
• Prayer times calculated on your device, adhan notifications, qibla, nawafil.
• Adhkar with counters and sources; 36 tafsirs, 33 translations, 17 hadith books with search.
• Khatma planner, Hijri calendar, the 99 Names and more. No ads, no subscriptions, no tracking.

## Review notes (لصق في App Review Information → Notes)
No login is needed. All Quran text, tafsir, hadith and adhkar are bundled and work offline. Location is optional (the user can pick a city manually) and is used only on the device for prayer times and qibla. Audio recitations stream from free public Quran archives (mp3quran.net, everyayah.com, quranicaudio.com, archive.org) and can be downloaded by the user for offline listening; background audio is used only for Quran playback. Reciter photos come from Wikimedia Commons under free licences and are credited on each reciter page.
